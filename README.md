# Aura Finance

Aura Finance is a proposed AI platform that helps individuals understand how
macroeconomic events affect their personal financial circumstances. It translates
complex economic information into personalized insights for income, business
operations, investment portfolios, and real estate holdings.

## Problem and audience

Economic information is widely available, but connecting it to personal financial
decisions remains difficult. Aura Finance aims to bridge that gap for consumers,
students, investors, and small business owners.

The initial MVP is intended for a weekend hackathon, with hackathon judges and
potential future users as its audience.

## Proposed MVP scope

- Capture a simplified financial profile using demo or user-provided inputs.
- Explain events such as inflation announcements, interest rate changes,
  employment reports, GDP trends, and housing market developments.
- Generate plain-language insights connecting those events to the user's profile.
- Demonstrate the experience through a React interface and FastAPI backend.

These features are planned, not yet implemented.

## Planned technology stack

| Component | Technology |
|---|---|
| Frontend | React |
| Backend | FastAPI |
| Database | SQLite |
| AI integration | OpenAI-compatible AI services |

## Assumptions and boundaries

- Development takes place during a weekend hackathon.
- Commercial deployment is outside the MVP scope.
- ROI focuses on educational value, hackathon competitiveness, and potential
  future commercialization rather than immediate financial returns.
- Business analysis covers the underlying problem, stakeholder needs,
  requirements, strategic positioning, financial considerations, and scope.

AI-generated insights are intended for educational purposes, not financial advice.
The prototype should use synthetic data rather than sensitive financial records.
